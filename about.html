Rotaract Bangalore East is a community-based club from Bangalore. We are bright, energetic, and motivated youngsters creating positive change in society.

UNITE: We are a family — no one gets left behind or forgotten.
RISE: With unity, we rise by learning from each other.
EMPOWER: With unity, we rise and empower the community around us.

Volunteer, collaborate, and support our events and projects. Let’s experience the joy of giving back. #rotaractblreast
